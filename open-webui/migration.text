The project was initially built on svelte 4. So to future proof,cleaner reactivity, smaller runtime and better performance we started to migrate it to svelte 5.

The Changes:

    New Reactivity Model (Runes):
        In Svelte 4, reactivity relies on $: labels and store syntax, which can sometimes feel magical and confusing to newcomers.
        Svelte 5’s runes ($state, $derived, $effect, etc.) make reactivity explicit and more predictable.

    Cleaner, Smaller Runtime:
        Svelte 5 produces smaller compiled output.
        More efficient updates, leading to faster apps with less JavaScript shipped to the browser.

    Consistency Across Ecosystem
        SvelteKit, adapters, and tools are moving toward Svelte 5 support as the default.
        Future libraries and community packages will optimize for Svelte 5 patterns.
